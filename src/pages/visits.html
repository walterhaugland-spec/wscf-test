What to expect

The ground is uneven in places, and the farm is outdoors in all weather.

What to wear and bring

  • Closed-toe shoes or boots. Mud is part of the deal.
  • Clothes that can get dirty, and layers for the weather
  • Water and sunscreen

Farm rules

  • Walk and use calm voices. Animals feel our energy.
  • Ask before touching any animal.
  • Stay with your group and follow your guide.
  • Wash your hands after working with animals.
  • Leave every gate the way you found it.
