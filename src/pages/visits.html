What to expect

The ground is uneven in places, and the farm is outdoors in all weather.

What to wear and bring

  • Closed-toe shoes or boots. Mud is part of the deal.
  • Clothes that can get dirty, and layers for the weather
  • Water and sunscreen

Farm rules

  • Walk and use calm voices. Animals feel our energy.
  • Ask before touching any animal.
  • Stay with your group and follow your guide.
  • Wash your hands after working with animals.
  • Leave every gate the way you found it.

Before your first visit

Waivers

Every visitor, volunteer, and program participant completes a waiver before their first visit. One per person. Parents or guardians sign for children under 18.

Common questions

Can I just drop by?
No. The farm is a private residence, so every visit is by appointment.
What if the weather is bad?
Most programs run rain or shine. We cancel when there is snow, or lightning within 5 miles of the farm. If we cancel, we contact you directly.
Can we take photos?
Photos of the animals and gardens are welcome. Please ask before photographing other people's children.
