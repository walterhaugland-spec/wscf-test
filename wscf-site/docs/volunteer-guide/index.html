Ask anything, anytime. No question is too small.

What we do

A working care farm, not a petting zoo

Kids, mostly ages 7 to 12, come here to feel calmer and more capable through real chores, animals with names, and adults who notice what they do well. Everyone on the farm works the same way, volunteers included.

01

Notice

See what needs doing. An empty water bucket, a gate left open, a kid who's stuck.

02

Get

Get the tools, help, or advice you need to do it right.

Go

Get after it.

With the kids

You don't need to be a teacher or a therapist

You need to be steady.

Use their names

Every kid here should feel known.

Let them do the work

Show once, then step back. A fed goat means more when they did it.

Notice out loud

Specific beats general. Say "You latched that gate on your own," not "Good job."

Slow down

Calm voice, easy movements. Kids and animals both match your pace.

If a kid is upset

Stay close and calm, and get the program lead. You don't have to fix it.

Always stay in sight. Never be alone with a child out of view of another adult.

With the animals

Animals feel our energy

Each one has its own rules. When you're not sure, ask a lead first.

  • Walk, and keep voices calm.
  • Ask before touching or feedingany animal.
  • Leave every gatethe way you found it.
  • Wash your handsafter animal work.
  • Hurt, sick, or loose?Tell a lead right away.

The non-negotiables

Keep it safe. Keep it private.

What's shared here stays here

Names, stories, and struggles aren't for retelling.

Photos

No photos of kids unless a lead says it's okay. Animals and gardens are fair game.

Phones away

During programs, your attention belongs to the kids and the animals.

Anyone hurt?

Tell a lead right away. First aid kit: [location]

Worried about a child's safety?

Tell Linda the same day. [Confirm reporting steps]

When in doubt, slow down, ask, and pay attention
