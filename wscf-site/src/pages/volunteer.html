Common questions

Do I need farm experience?
No. You'll get an orientation on your first visit. Tell us what skills you bring and we'll put them to use.
How old do volunteers need to be?
Teens 13 and older can volunteer on their own or join the Teen Leadership Crew. Kids 5 and older can volunteer with a parent.
What should I wear?
Closed-toe shoes or boots, clothes that can get dirty, and gloves if you have them. Bring water.
Do volunteers need a waiver?
Yes. Every volunteer signs a waiver before their first visit. Find the waivers.

Ready to lend a hand?
